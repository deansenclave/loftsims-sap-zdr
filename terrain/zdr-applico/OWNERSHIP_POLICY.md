## Required release controls

1. Preserve owner/originator attribution in artifact manifests, architectural documents, and release metadata.
2. Record creation date when known, last modification date, artifact version, and associated Git commit or release tag. Never invent historical dates or authorship.
3. Maintain a traceable change history and link changed requirements, assertions/evals, designs, implementations and test evidence.
4. Include appropriate copyright notices in eligible original source code, documentation and release packages, without falsely claiming ownership of third-party materials.
5. Preserve third-party attribution, license obligations, and contributor provenance separately from project ownership claims.
6. Treat missing or contradictory ownership/provenance metadata as a release validation failure; record exceptions for owner approval.
7. Do not overwrite immutable historical release snapshots solely to retrofit notices; attach accurate supplemental metadata instead.
8. #HumansOnTop: final release and ownership decisions remain with Sudheendra Pai.
